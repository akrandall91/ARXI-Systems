About ARXI

We make useful technology you can hold.

ARXI Systems combines 3D printing, electronics, LEDs, NFC, sensors and software into finished products — things that light up, display information, sense a room and respond.

Designed, printed and built in North Carolina.

ARXI is run by Andrew Randall. Every piece is designed, 3D-printed, wired, tested and finished here, and every product page says plainly whether it’s available, built to order, or still in the Lab.

  • What it does comes first. Specs come second.
  • Finished enclosures, not exposed circuit boards.
  • No subscriptions forced onto products.
