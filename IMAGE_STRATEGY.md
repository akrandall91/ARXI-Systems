# ARXI Systems Image Strategy

Audit date: 2026-07-09

## Strategic Direction

ARXI imagery should communicate accountable smart infrastructure: real civic settings, practical public-sector deployment, system coordination, privacy-first field intelligence, and manufacturer readiness. The site should not feel like a generic consulting site, a startup buzzword page, or a solar-only company.

The implemented system uses:

- Generated custom images for the most precise ARXI concepts.
- Retained stock images for real-world scenario grounding.
- Dark overlays, route/grid treatments, and consistent cropping to integrate images with the ARXI visual language.
