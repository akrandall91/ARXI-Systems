Frequently asked questions

Before you order.

Do I need an app to use it?

No app is normally required for compatible smartphones. Hold the phone near the tap symbol and it opens the destination automatically.

Why isn't my phone reading it?

NFC performance depends on your phone model and where its NFC antenna is located. Hold your phone near the tap symbol and give it a moment — you may need to move it slightly to find the right spot.

Can I put it on a metal counter?

Metal surfaces can interfere with NFC performance. For the most reliable results, use the stand on an ordinary countertop or desk.

Does it accept payments directly?

No. A payment or tipping destination simply opens the payment page you supply — the stand itself never processes or stores payment-card information.

Is this a wireless charger?

No — this is a tap-to-connect NFC stand, not a wireless charger.

Can I change the destination link later?

The embedded tag isn't designed to be swapped out, but give us a call — we can walk you through reprogramming it yourself, or take care of it for you.

Can I add my logo or custom artwork?

Check "Add my logo or custom artwork" while building your stand and we'll follow up to work out sizing, placement, and details before production. For a fully custom look — matching landing page and branding included — request a custom quote.

How exact does my URL need to be?

Provide the exact, live link you want the stand to open. We test every link before your order is completed, so make sure it's live before you submit it.
