Questions

Before you order.

Does it work with every phone?

Nearly all modern iPhones and Android phones read tap cards without an app. The printed QR code covers the rest.

Can I change the link myself?

Yes. A how-to card comes with every order; it takes under a minute with a free phone app. Or ask us and we’ll do it.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You buy it once and own it.

Can I order different links on different cards?

Yes — add the links in the notes and we’ll program each card.
