AG-003 Scenario Systems

What can be created together.

Each scenario starts with a public problem, then shapes a practical system around site conditions, stakeholders, pilot evidence, and deployment logic.

Transit Stop Modernization

Starting problem
Riders wait in exposed areas with limited lighting, limited arrival information, and no clear sense of safety or service quality.
What can be created together
Solar lighting, smart shelter options, real-time arrival displays, ADA messaging, alert lighting, non-camera presence sensing, telemetry, and maintenance status.
Why it matters
The stop is where public confidence in transit is won or lost.
Stakeholders
Transit, planning, procurement, operations, accessibility, public safety.
Pilot outcome
A measurable stop upgrade model that can scale corridor by corridor.

Parks, Trails & Public Spaces

Starting problem
Parks and trails need safety, visibility, information, and maintenance awareness without overbuilding or trenching power everywhere.
What can be created together
Solar lighting, wayfinding displays, emergency message nodes, air quality or heat sensing, non-invasive presence data, trailhead information, and maintenance telemetry.
Why it matters
Good public space feels cared for. Poorly lit or poorly maintained space feels abandoned.
Stakeholders
Parks, sustainability, public works, recreation, community safety, grants.
Pilot outcome
A low-disruption pilot that improves comfort, visibility, and operational awareness.

Downtown Corridor / Smart District

Starting problem
A city wants a smarter corridor without disconnected poles, displays, sensors, and vendors fighting each other.
What can be created together
Smart poles, solar-assisted lighting, public information displays, environmental sensing, wayfinding, public Wi-Fi support, emergency alerts, and asset monitoring.
Why it matters
Corridor modernization works best when infrastructure is designed as a system.
Stakeholders
Economic development, transportation, public works, IT, sustainability, downtown partnerships.
Pilot outcome
A phased smart-corridor blueprint with clear technology roles and expansion logic.

Airport / Campus Mobility Nodes

Starting problem
People need clear information, safe waiting areas, and reliable mobility connections across complex sites.
What can be created together
Passenger information displays, lighting, solar backup power, transportation signage, shuttle/bus information, presence-aware activation, and secure-zone-friendly technology.
Why it matters
Airports and campuses are judged by movement, clarity, safety, and uptime.
Stakeholders
Facilities, operations, transportation, security, sustainability, IT.
Pilot outcome
A mobility-node package that can be replicated across terminals, lots, campus stops, or shuttle hubs.

Public Works & Resilience

Starting problem
Infrastructure teams need field visibility, backup power, and faster awareness of site conditions.
What can be created together
Solar-powered telemetry nodes, emergency message displays, environmental monitoring, battery-backed power, asset dashboards, and maintenance reporting.
Why it matters
Resilience is knowing what is happening in the field before the complaint arrives.
Stakeholders
Public works, emergency management, utilities, sustainability, IT, procurement.
Pilot outcome
A resilience node or corridor test with operational reporting.

Civic Intelligence & Planning

Starting problem
Agencies need to decide where to pilot, how to justify funding, and how to prioritize sites.
What can be created together
Public data overlays, solar feasibility signals, context mapping, grant alignment, transit opportunity analysis, site scoring, and pilot-readiness reports.
Why it matters
Better planning makes pilots easier to defend, fund, and scale.
Stakeholders
Planning, grants, sustainability, transit, GIS, procurement, leadership.
Pilot outcome
A site-priority model that helps decide where infrastructure should go first.

AG-007 Agency FAQ

Questions agencies ask early.

Does ARXI manufacture everything?

No. ARXI may build, source, represent, or integrate depending on the project. The value is making the system fit the agency's need and coordinating the right path.

Can ARXI support pilots?

Yes. ARXI can help shape pilot sites, success criteria, data rules, responsibilities, vendor coordination, and the evidence needed to decide whether to scale.

Can ARXI work with existing vendors?

Yes. If existing vendors fit the site and procurement context, ARXI can help coordinate around them instead of forcing replacement.

Can ARXI help define specs before procurement?

ARXI can help clarify needs, product categories, responsibilities, documentation, and pilot requirements before a formal procurement path is selected.

Does ARXI use cameras or facial recognition?

ARXI's preferred smart infrastructure model is privacy-first and does not require facial recognition or unnecessary identity tracking.

Can this support grants or sustainability goals?

Yes. ARXI can help frame projects around safety, resilience, mobility, climate, energy, accessibility, and operational outcomes.

What types of agencies are a fit?

Transit agencies, municipalities, parks, airports, campuses, emergency management, public works, sustainability teams, and planning departments.
