Common questions

Before you order

Will I see a proof before production?

ARXI Systems reviews your source image and configuration before production. If an adjustment is necessary, we contact you using the information submitted with the order. Production does not begin until required artwork questions are resolved.

How long will my order take?

Timing depends on product type, quantity, lighting complexity, proof approval, and the current production queue. Your expected production window is confirmed before payment or before production begins. Tell us about a deadline when ordering so feasibility can be checked.

How does shipping work?

Shipping availability, cost, and delivery timing are confirmed for the destination before the order is finalized. Oversized pieces and coordinated installations may require a custom delivery plan.

Can I request changes?

Use the personalization notes to describe colors, wording, crop preferences, or other important details. Necessary production questions are handled during proof review. Changes requested after approval may affect price or timing.

Can a custom order be returned or canceled?

Because each piece is made for a specific customer, custom-order cancellations and returns are reviewed based on production status and the nature of the issue. Contact ARXI Systems immediately at andrew@akrandall.com if something is wrong.

What files should I upload?

Use the clearest, highest-resolution original available. JPG and PNG work well for most photos and artwork. Avoid screenshots, heavily compressed social-media downloads, and images where the important subject is very small.

How is payment handled?

Standard configurations use secure Square-hosted checkout. Custom and oversized work is reviewed and quoted before a payment request is sent. ARXI Systems does not ask customers to send card details by email.

What is included?

Applicable physical products include the selected table or wall mount. Illuminated products include the lighting and power hardware described by the selected tier. The order summary shows any per-piece upgrades and one-time connected-system fee.

Does this upload-and-proof process apply to the Tap Stand too?

No — the Tap Stand doesn't use an uploaded photo or a proof step. Choose your format and colors and tell us the destination link; we test the link before your stand is completed. See the Tap Stand page for its own build steps and FAQ.
