The most important thing to understand

A lithophane is made to come alive with light.

The image is sculpted into a thin physical panel. It can look quiet and almost hidden when the light is off. When light passes through it, the different thicknesses reveal the faces, details, and depth.

Light off

A real sculpted object

The picture is physically formed into the panel. In normal room light, you can see the raised detail and texture.

Light on

The photograph is revealed

Backlighting brings out contrast, faces, clothing, and fine image detail. This change is the point of the piece.

Without backlight

Subtle in your hand

Even a small panel holds a surprising amount of physical detail.

With backlight

Clear when light passes through

A nearby lamp demonstrates the effect; finished illuminated products include the lighting and power hardware selected in your order.

Classic vs. Color: Classic uses light and sculpted depth for a timeless monochrome image. Color adds a full-color image layer while keeping the dimensional, illuminated effect.

What does “set” mean?

Several pieces can share one coordinated experience.

A set does not mean three copies have to look the same. It can combine different photos, orientations, sizes, and even product types to tell one story across a shelf, wall, event, or room.

A real three-piece example

Two memories. One name. One display.

This arrangement combines portrait photography with a graphic name lightbox. The pieces still stand on their own, but together they feel intentionally designed.

Connected means upgraded lighting is configured as one coordinated system. Independent means each piece operates separately.

Operate together

A coordinated color across all pieces creates one room-wide lighting moment.

Still beautiful independently

Each piece keeps its own image and purpose. A set is coordination, not duplication.
