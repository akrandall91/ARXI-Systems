# ARXI Stock Media Sourcing Manifest

This directory is wired into `index.html`. Drop optimized, rights-cleared assets into these exact filenames.

Do not use copyrighted manufacturer product images unless ARXI has explicit rights.

## Hero

- `arxi-hero-infrastructure.webm`
- `arxi-hero-infrastructure.jpg`

Recommended sources: Pexels city night bus video, Pexels public transport video, Pixabay city night lights video, Pixabay smart city video.

Style: dark, cinematic, civic, transit or street-lighting atmosphere. Use an overlay for text contrast. Avoid recognizable manufacturer branding.

## Flagship Smart Infrastructure

- `flagship-smart-shelter.jpg`

Recommended sources: real ARXI/product/project photos first; otherwise Pexels bus stop/public transport/streetlights, Pixabay bus shelter imagery, or a custom generated smart shelter render.

Style: premium shelter, solar canopy or lighting emphasis, digital display feel, civic environment. Use a custom render if stock feels generic or legally ambiguous.

## Final CTA

- `final-cta-infrastructure.jpg`

Recommended source: city lights, infrastructure corridor, streetlight, or public-space route at dusk/night.

Style: quiet, premium, darker exposure with enough structure for route-line/radar overlay.

## Optimization Notes

- Hero video: WebM, muted loop, target under 3-5 MB.
- JPG/WebP images: 1600-2200 px wide for hero/final/flagship, 900-1200 px wide for use cases.
- Strip metadata before deployment when possible.
- Keep filenames stable so no code changes are needed after sourcing.
